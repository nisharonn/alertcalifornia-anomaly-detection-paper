\opening{To whom it may concern,}

I am writing to submit a manuscript titled \textit{Quality of Service Anomaly Detection in Wildfire Monitoring Networks} for consideration to the \textit{Journal of Remote Sensing}. This article focuses on development and characterization of a novel method to evaluate visual anomalies impacing the ability of ALERTCalifornia cameras to monitor for natural hazards. This method comprises both a heuristic for human reviewers to evaluate the imagery with, as well as a new ML model architecture that fits this task.

This material has not been previously published.
